Black out an IBAN or address in a document

Free

Before a statement or contract goes out, not everything on it should be readable. Vanish finds IBANs, tax IDs, addresses and more, and burns the bars in for good.

How it works

  1. Open the vault, tap the photo or PDF, then “…” at the top right and Black out.
  2. Vanish reads the text on your device and marks suggestions in orange, such as an IBAN, tax ID, phone number, address or ID number.
  3. Tap a suggestion to remove it. Black out your own spots by tapping a word, double-tapping a line or dragging over an area.
  4. Undo takes back the last bar, and All suggestions accepts every orange spot.
  5. Tap Save and choose Save as new version. The original then stays in the vault.

Good to know

  • Blacking out is free and works on photos and PDFs.
  • The bars are rendered into the image or page. No selectable text remains underneath.
  • The suggestions are a help, not a guarantee. Look at the result before sending.
  • Replace original deletes the unredacted version. This can’t be undone.
