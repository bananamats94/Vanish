What Vanish does

A camera with an expiry date

Pick an hour, tonight, tomorrow or the end of a meeting. The ring around the shutter shows how long the photo lives. Then it is gone, without you thinking about it.

Clear out old screenshots

Vanish finds screenshots and images with codes in your photo library, right on your iPhone. It moves them into the vault with an expiry, and iOS deletes the originals once you confirm. Free for everyone, new in 1.6.

Hand over files, sealed

Send any file as an encrypted .vanish file through any messenger, AirDrop or email. You decide until when it opens, how many times, and whether it may be forwarded.

  • Open a .vanish fileA .vanish file is an encrypted handover. You open it with the Vanish app on an iPhone or iPad. It is free and needs no account.
  • Send a copy of your ID securelyA landlord, a bank, a new job: often a copy of your ID has to go out, but it shouldn’t sit in a chat forever. Vanish scans both sides, stamps a purpose note on it and sends the copy with an expiry.
  • Send only some pages of a PDFOften someone only needs two pages of a long PDF, like a lease or a statement. In Vanish you split those pages out and send only them.

What Vanish knows about you

  • Vanish collects no data. There is no account, no server of ours and no third-party library in the app.
  • Your vault is never backed up to iCloud. Its key stays in your iPhone’s Keychain and does not leave the device.
  • Vanish only goes online through Apple services you trigger yourself: iCloud for your settings, map search for places, and purchases.
  • This website sets no cookies and loads no tracking scripts.

What no app can do: stop someone from photographing the screen with a second phone. Vanish does not claim otherwise.
