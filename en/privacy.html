2. What is Vanish?

Vanish is a camera app that lets you take photos that are automatically deleted after a time you specify. Perfect for temporary photos like parking spots, price tags, or passwords.

3. What Data Do We Process?

Vanish stores all data exclusively on your device:

  • Photos: Saved in the Vanish album in your photo library
  • Timer settings: Your custom deletion times
  • Preset configurations: Names, icons, and time settings

Important: We have no access to your photos or other data. Everything stays on your device.

4. Permissions

Vanish requires the following permissions:

  • Camera: To take photos
  • Photos: To save images to the Vanish album and delete them when they expire
  • Background Refresh: To delete expired photos even in the background

You can revoke these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

5. Data Sharing

We do not share your data with third parties. There is:

  • No advertising
  • No tracking services
  • No cloud synchronization
  • No analytics tools

6. Your Rights

You have the following rights at any time:

  • Access: All data is visible on your device
  • Deletion: Delete the app and all data is removed
  • Control: You decide when photos are deleted
