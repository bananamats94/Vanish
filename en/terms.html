2. Service Description

Vanish is a camera app with automatic photo deletion:

  • Take photos with a predefined deletion timer
  • Automatic deletion after the set time expires
  • OCR text recognition for captured images
  • Customizable timer presets
  • Widget and Siri integration

3. Important Notice About Deletion

Warning: Automatic deletion is irreversible. Deleted photos cannot be recovered. Make sure to save important photos before the timer expires.

Deletion may be delayed if:

  • The app cannot refresh in the background
  • The device is turned off
  • Background refresh has been disabled
